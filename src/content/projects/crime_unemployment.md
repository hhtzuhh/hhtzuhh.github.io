# Analysis of Crime and Unemployment Pre and Post-Pandemic

## Abstract

The emergence and widespread effects of COVID-19 has had, and continues to have, a profound impact on both the healthcare outcomes of humans and worldwide economies. This study examines the multifaceted impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on the US economy from 2018-2023, analyzing unemployment rates, stock market indices (DJIA, NASDAQ, S&P 500), LinkedIn job postings, and crime rates to understand pandemic effects on the economy.
