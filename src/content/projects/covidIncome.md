# THE IMPACT OF HOUSEHOLD INCOME ON COVID-19 DEATHS

## Abstract

With Covid-19 threatening globally since 2020, there are still many hidden information about how demographic environment interacts with the Covid-19 disease. By joining the 2020 US Census and Covid-19 datasets in the database, we were able to analyze many attributes and found that the Covid-19 deaths is significantly related to the household income.
