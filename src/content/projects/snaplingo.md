# SnapLingo
SnapLingo is an intelligent AI-powered support system specifically designed to help Taiwanese high school students excel in the English composition section of the General Scholastic Ability Test (GSAT/學測).

## What it does
SnapLingo provides students with an online platform to practice for the college entrance test. Powered by an AI tutor and scaffolding teaching techniques, it guides students step-by-step to express their own ideas in writing.

Leveraging AI's flexibility, students can write what they truly want to express rather than being constrained by traditional model essays. The AI grading system evaluates student compositions based on the College Entrance Examination Center's four-tier scoring rubric and collects practice records. These records can be used to recommend personalized review materials and provide tailored study guidance.
